# AP-SEC-003 Staff Access, Restricted Items, and Approvals

## 1. Purpose and Scope  
This policy establishes procedures for managing staff access to restricted areas and the handling of restricted items within AeroPolicy Airport. It outlines the responsibilities of staff, managers, and security personnel in ensuring compliance with safety and security standards. The policy applies to all staff members, including those working in landside, airside, and baggage make-up areas. It also governs the use, transport, and storage of restricted items and defines the approval process for their movement within the airport.

## 2. Staff Access Zones  
Staff access to airport areas is restricted to designated zones based on job responsibilities and security clearance. Landside areas are accessible to all staff with valid credentials, while airside areas require additional authorization and security clearance. The baggage make-up area is restricted to authorized personnel only. Access to these zones is monitored and controlled to ensure the safety and security of the airport environment. Staff must adhere to all access protocols and report any unauthorized entry or suspicious activity.

## 3. Restricted Items  
Restricted items include tools, blades, lithium batteries, and flammable liquids, among others. These items pose potential risks to safety and security and are subject to strict handling and storage procedures. Staff must be aware of the types of restricted items and their associated risks. All restricted items must be stored securely and transported only with proper authorization. Staff are required to follow established protocols to prevent unauthorized access or misuse of restricted items.

## 4. Approval Procedure for Restricted Items  
Approval to carry a restricted item airside must be granted in writing by the Security Duty Manager before the item passes the staff screening point. A Requesting Staff Member must submit a written request detailing the item, its purpose, and the intended location of use. The Line Manager must endorse the request to confirm its necessity and compliance with safety standards. The Security Duty Manager reviews and approves the request based on risk assessment and operational requirements. All approvals must be documented and retained for audit purposes.

## 5. Restricted Items Found in Baggage  
A restricted item found in a checked bag must be handled in accordance with AP-BAG-001. The item must be removed from the baggage and secured for further review. The incident must be reported under AP-INC-002, including details of the item, the baggage, and the location of discovery. All relevant staff must follow the procedures outlined in these policies to ensure proper handling and documentation. The Security Duty Manager is responsible for coordinating the response and ensuring compliance with applicable regulations.

## 6. Records and Review  
All records related to restricted items, including approval requests, incident reports, and handling procedures, must be maintained for a minimum of two years. These records are subject to periodic review by the Security Duty Manager and senior management to ensure compliance with this policy and related procedures. Staff must ensure that all documentation is accurate, complete, and accessible for audit or investigation. Any discrepancies or non-compliance must be reported immediately and addressed through appropriate corrective actions.
