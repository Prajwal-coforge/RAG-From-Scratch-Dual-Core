# AP-SEC-003 Staff Access, Restricted Items, and Approvals

## 1. Purpose and Scope  
This policy establishes procedures for managing staff access to restricted areas and the handling of restricted items within AeroPolicy Airport. It defines the zones where staff may operate, the types of items that require special handling, and the approval process for accessing these items. The policy applies to all staff members, including those working in landside, airside, and baggage make-up areas. It ensures compliance with safety, security, and operational standards while maintaining the integrity of airport operations.

## 2. Staff Access Zones  
Staff access is restricted to designated zones based on job responsibilities. Landside areas include public concourses, retail spaces, and service counters. Airside areas are restricted to authorized personnel with appropriate clearance and include runways, taxiways, and aircraft maintenance zones. The baggage make-up area is a controlled zone where only designated staff may operate. Access to these zones is governed by internal security protocols and is subject to regular review and audit to ensure compliance with safety and security requirements.

## 3. Restricted Items  
Restricted items include tools, blades, lithium batteries, and flammable liquids, among others. These items pose potential risks to safety and security and require special handling. Staff must be aware of the types of items that are restricted and understand the procedures for requesting access to them. The presence of restricted items in any area of the airport may affect operational safety and must be reported immediately. Staff are required to follow all procedures outlined in this policy to ensure compliance and mitigate risks.

## 4. Approval Procedure for Restricted Items  
Approval to carry a restricted item airside must be granted in writing by the Security Duty Manager before the item passes the staff screening point. A Requesting Staff Member must submit a written request detailing the item, its purpose, and the intended location of use. The Line Manager must endorse the request to confirm its necessity and compliance with operational requirements. The Security Duty Manager reviews the request and grants final approval based on risk assessment and policy compliance. All requests must be documented and retained for audit purposes.

## 5. Restricted Items Found in Baggage  
A restricted item found in a checked bag is handled under AP-BAG-001, which outlines procedures for identifying, isolating, and reporting such items. The discovery of a restricted item in baggage must be reported under AP-INC-002, which details the process for documenting and escalating incidents. Staff are required to follow these procedures to ensure the safety of all passengers and staff while maintaining the integrity of the airport’s security protocols.

## 6. Records and Review  
All requests for restricted items must be recorded in a centralized log for audit and review. The Security Duty Manager is responsible for maintaining and reviewing these records to ensure compliance with this policy and related procedures. Regular audits are conducted to assess adherence to the approval process and identify areas for improvement. Staff are required to maintain accurate and up-to-date records and to report any discrepancies or non-compliance promptly. Records are retained for a minimum of two years and may be reviewed by authorized personnel as needed.
