# AP-SEC-003 Staff Access, Restricted Items, and Approvals

## 1. Purpose and Scope  
This policy establishes guidelines for managing staff access to restricted areas and controlling the handling of restricted items within AeroPolicy Airport. It defines the roles and procedures for requesting access to airside and landside zones, as well as the approval process for carrying restricted items. The policy applies to all staff members, contractors, and third-party personnel who require access to restricted areas or may handle restricted items. Compliance with this policy ensures the safety, security, and operational integrity of the airport.

## 2. Staff Access Zones  
Staff access to restricted areas is strictly controlled and limited to authorized personnel. The landside area includes all zones accessible to the public and non-secure staff. The airside area encompasses the secure zones accessible only to authorized airside personnel. The baggage make-up area is a restricted zone where only designated staff may operate. Access to these zones is granted based on role, training, and approval, and unauthorized entry is prohibited.

## 3. Restricted Items  
Restricted items include tools, blades, lithium batteries, and flammable liquids, among others. These items are subject to specific handling and storage procedures to mitigate safety risks. Staff must be aware of which items are classified as restricted and understand the procedures for their use, transport, and storage. Unauthorized possession or movement of restricted items is a violation of this policy and may result in disciplinary action.

## 4. Approval Procedure for Restricted Items  
Approval to carry a restricted item airside must be granted in writing by the Security Duty Manager before the item passes the staff screening point. A Requesting Staff Member must submit a written request detailing the item, its purpose, and the intended location of use. The Line Manager must endorse the request to confirm its necessity and compliance with operational requirements. The Security Duty Manager reviews and approves the request based on risk assessment and policy compliance. All approvals are documented and retained for audit purposes.

## 5. Restricted Items Found in Baggage  
If a restricted item is discovered in a checked bag, it is handled in accordance with AP-BAG-001. The item is removed from the baggage and secured for inspection. The incident is reported under AP-INC-002 to ensure proper documentation and follow-up. Staff are required to notify security immediately upon discovery of any restricted item in baggage. All actions taken are in line with applicable safety and security regulations.

## 6. Records and Review  
All requests, approvals, and incident reports related to restricted items are recorded and maintained in a secure database. These records are reviewed periodically to ensure compliance with this policy and to identify any trends or issues requiring corrective action. Staff are required to maintain accurate and up-to-date records of their access and handling of restricted items. The Security Duty Manager oversees the review process and ensures the integrity of the records.
