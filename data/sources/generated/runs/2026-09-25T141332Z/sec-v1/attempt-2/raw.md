# AP-SEC-003 Staff Access, Restricted Items, and Approvals

## 1. Purpose and Scope  
This policy establishes procedures for managing staff access to restricted areas and the handling of restricted items within AeroPolicy Airport. It defines the zones where staff may operate, the categories of items that require special handling, and the approval process for accessing these items. The policy ensures compliance with security and safety standards, minimizes risks associated with restricted items, and provides a clear framework for authorizing and monitoring their use. It applies to all staff members and applies to all restricted items, including tools, blades, lithium batteries, and flammable liquids.

## 2. Staff Access Zones  
Staff access is restricted to designated zones based on operational roles. Landside access is available to all staff performing ground operations, while airside access is limited to authorized personnel with appropriate credentials. The baggage make-up area is restricted to baggage handlers and security personnel. Access to these zones is controlled through physical barriers, electronic systems, and personnel verification. Staff must not enter restricted zones without proper authorization. Unauthorized access may result in disciplinary action. All staff are required to be familiar with the boundaries and access protocols of each zone.

## 3. Restricted Items  
Restricted items include tools, blades, lithium batteries, and flammable liquids, which pose potential safety or security risks. These items may be required for operational purposes but must be managed in accordance with this policy. Staff must not carry restricted items without prior approval. The presence of such items in unapproved locations may lead to immediate removal and disciplinary action. All staff are responsible for identifying and reporting restricted items to the appropriate authorities. Proper storage and handling procedures must be followed to prevent accidents or unauthorized use.

## 4. Approval Procedure for Restricted Items  
Approval to carry a restricted item airside must be granted in writing by the Security Duty Manager before the item passes the staff screening point. A Requesting Staff Member must submit a written request detailing the item, its purpose, and the intended location of use. The Line Manager must endorse the request to confirm its necessity and compliance with operational requirements. The Security Duty Manager reviews and approves the request based on risk assessment and security protocols. All approvals must be documented and retained for audit purposes. Unauthorized possession or use of restricted items is strictly prohibited.

## 5. Restricted Items Found in Baggage  
If a restricted item is found in a checked bag, it is handled in accordance with AP-BAG-001. The item must be removed from the baggage and secured for further inspection. The incident must be reported under AP-INC-002 to ensure proper documentation and follow-up. Staff are required to notify security immediately upon discovery of any restricted items in baggage. The handling of such items is conducted in a manner that ensures passenger safety and compliance with regulatory requirements. All actions taken must be consistent with the procedures outlined in the relevant policies.

## 6. Records and Review  
All requests for restricted items, along with their approvals and outcomes, must be recorded in a secure and accessible database. These records are reviewed periodically to ensure compliance with this policy and to identify any trends or issues. The Security Duty Manager is responsible for maintaining and updating the records. Access to these records is restricted to authorized personnel only. Any discrepancies or unauthorized activities must be reported promptly. The records serve as a critical tool for auditing, training, and improving operational procedures. Regular reviews ensure the policy remains effective and up to date.
