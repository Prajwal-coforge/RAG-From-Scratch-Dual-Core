# AP-SEC-003 Staff Access, Restricted Items, and Approvals

## 1. Purpose and Scope  
This policy establishes procedures for managing staff access to restricted areas and the handling of restricted items within AeroPolicy Airport. It defines the access zones available to staff, outlines the categories of restricted items, and sets the approval process for their use. The policy ensures compliance with safety and security standards while facilitating the efficient operation of airport services. All staff members must adhere to these procedures when accessing restricted areas or requesting the use of restricted items.

## 2. Staff Access Zones  
Staff access is divided into three zones: landside, airside, and baggage make-up area. Landside access is available to all staff for general operations. Airside access is restricted to authorized personnel with proper credentials. The baggage make-up area is accessible only to designated staff involved in baggage handling. Access to these zones is governed by individual roles and responsibilities, and unauthorized entry is strictly prohibited.

## 3. Restricted Items  
Restricted items include tools, blades, lithium batteries, and flammable liquids. These items pose potential safety hazards and require special handling. Staff must be aware of the types of restricted items and their associated risks. The use or possession of restricted items is subject to approval and must comply with applicable regulations. Staff are responsible for ensuring that restricted items are stored and transported in accordance with established procedures.

## 4. Approval Procedure for Restricted Items  
Approval to carry a restricted item airside must be granted in writing by the Security Duty Manager before the item passes the staff screening point. A requesting staff member must submit a written request detailing the item, its purpose, and the intended location of use. The request must be endorsed by the line manager, who verifies the necessity and compliance with safety protocols. The Security Duty Manager reviews the request and grants final approval. All steps must be completed prior to the item being transported to airside areas.

## 5. Restricted Items Found in Baggage  
If a restricted item is found in a checked bag, it must be handled in accordance with AP-BAG-001. The item is removed from the baggage and secured for further inspection. The incident must be reported under AP-INC-002, ensuring that all relevant parties are informed and appropriate actions are taken. Staff are required to follow these procedures to maintain the integrity of the security process and ensure compliance with regulatory requirements.

## 6. Records and Review  
All requests for restricted items must be documented and retained for review. Records include the requesting staff member’s details, the item description, and the approval chain. These records are reviewed periodically to ensure compliance with this policy and to identify any procedural improvements. Staff are responsible for maintaining accurate and complete records, and unauthorized alterations or deletions are not permitted.
