# AP-BAG-001 Staff Baggage Handling and Escalation

## 1. Purpose and Scope

This policy establishes the procedures and responsibilities for the handling of checked baggage at AeroPolicy Airport. It outlines the roles of staff involved in baggage operations, the standards for baggage handling, and the protocols for incident escalation and restricted item management. The policy applies to all baggage handlers, baggage duty supervisors, and ramp leads during the processing of baggage between the check-in belts, the baggage make-up area, and aircraft loading.

## 2. Roles and Responsibilities

The baggage handler is responsible for the safe and efficient movement of checked baggage through the baggage handling system. This includes loading and unloading baggage from conveyor belts, ensuring proper placement on carts, and maintaining the integrity of baggage during transit. The baggage duty supervisor oversees the operations of baggage handlers and ensures compliance with all handling standards. The ramp lead is responsible for coordinating baggage operations with aircraft crews and ensuring that all baggage is loaded and secured in accordance with safety and operational requirements.

## 3. Handling Standards

All baggage handlers must ensure that baggage is handled with care to prevent damage, leakage, or loss. When a baggage handler finds a damaged, leaking, or unattended checked bag in the baggage make-up area, the handler must escalate the incident to the Baggage Duty Supervisor within 30 minutes of discovery and must report it under AP-INC-002. Fragile items must be clearly marked and handled with extra care to prevent breakage. Heavy bags must be secured properly to avoid tipping or damage during transit. All baggage must be tagged correctly and verified for accuracy before being loaded onto aircraft.

## 4. Baggage Incident Escalation

In the event of a baggage incident, the baggage handler must immediately inform the Baggage Duty Supervisor and provide a detailed description of the incident. The Baggage Duty Supervisor is responsible for documenting the incident and initiating the appropriate corrective actions. If the incident involves a safety or security concern, the supervisor must escalate the matter to the ramp lead for further action. All incidents must be reported in accordance with AP-INC-002, and appropriate records must be maintained for review and analysis.

## 5. Restricted Items Found in Baggage

If a restricted item is found in a bag during baggage handling, the baggage handler must immediately stop further processing of the bag and notify the Baggage Duty Supervisor. The bag must not be handled further until it is approved or removed under the restricted-items approval procedure in AP-SEC-003. The Baggage Duty Supervisor is responsible for ensuring that the bag is secured and that all relevant authorities are notified in accordance with the procedures outlined in AP-SEC-003.

## 6. Records and Review

All baggage incidents and restricted item findings must be recorded in the incident log for review and analysis. The incident log must be maintained by the Baggage Duty Supervisor and made available for inspection by authorized personnel. This policy will be reviewed annually to ensure its continued relevance and effectiveness. Any changes to the policy will be communicated to all relevant staff and implemented in accordance with established procedures.
