# AP-BAG-001 Staff Baggage Handling and Escalation

## 1. Purpose and Scope

This policy establishes the procedures and responsibilities for the handling of checked baggage at AeroPolicy Airport, from the check-in belts through the baggage make-up area and onto aircraft loading. It ensures the safe, efficient, and compliant movement of baggage while maintaining the integrity of the airport’s security and operational standards. This policy applies to all staff involved in the baggage handling process, including Baggage Handlers, Baggage Duty Supervisors, and Ramp Leads.

## 2. Roles and Responsibilities

The Baggage Handler is responsible for the physical handling of baggage, including loading, unloading, and sorting. The Baggage Duty Supervisor oversees the operations of the baggage handling team, ensuring compliance with procedures and addressing any issues that arise. The Ramp Lead is responsible for coordinating baggage handling activities with aircraft operations and ensuring that all baggage is loaded and unloaded in accordance with established protocols. All staff must follow these roles and responsibilities to maintain operational efficiency and safety.

## 3. Handling Standards

All baggage must be handled with care to prevent damage, loss, or misrouting. Baggage Handlers must inspect each bag for damage, leaking, or unattended status before handling. When a baggage handler finds a damaged, leaking, or unattended checked bag in the baggage make-up area, the handler must escalate the incident to the Baggage Duty Supervisor within 30 minutes of discovery and must report it under AP-INC-002. Baggage Handlers must also ensure that all bags are properly tagged and that heavy or fragile items are handled with appropriate care. Heavy bags must be secured to prevent movement during transit, and fragile items must be clearly marked and handled with extra caution to avoid breakage.

## 4. Baggage Incident Escalation

In the event of a baggage incident, such as damage, loss, or delay, the Baggage Duty Supervisor must immediately investigate the cause and take corrective action. All incidents must be reported in writing and recorded in the incident log. If the incident involves a security or safety concern, the Ramp Lead must be notified promptly to ensure that appropriate measures are taken. All staff must be trained to recognize and report incidents in a timely manner to ensure the integrity of the baggage handling process.

## 5. Restricted Items Found in Baggage

If a restricted item is found in a bag during the baggage handling process, the Baggage Handler must immediately stop handling the bag and notify the Baggage Duty Supervisor. The bag must not be further handled until it is approved or removed under the restricted-items approval procedure in AP-SEC-003. All staff must be aware of the types of restricted items and the procedures for their identification and removal to ensure compliance with security regulations.

## 6. Records and Review

All baggage handling incidents must be documented in the incident log, including details of the incident, the actions taken, and the personnel involved. The incident log must be reviewed annually to assess the effectiveness of the baggage handling procedures and to identify any areas for improvement. All staff must be made aware of the incident log and its importance in maintaining operational transparency and accountability. The policy will be reviewed annually to ensure its continued relevance and effectiveness.
