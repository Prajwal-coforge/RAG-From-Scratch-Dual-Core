# AP-BAG-001 Staff Baggage Handling and Escalation

## 1. Purpose and Scope

This policy establishes the procedures and responsibilities for the handling of checked baggage at AeroPolicy Airport. It outlines the roles of staff involved in baggage operations, the standards for baggage handling, and the protocols for incident escalation and restricted item management. The policy applies to all baggage handlers, baggage duty supervisors, and ramp leads during the processing of baggage between the check-in belts, the baggage make-up area, and aircraft loading.

## 2. Roles and Responsibilities

The baggage handler is responsible for the safe and efficient movement of checked baggage through the baggage handling system. This includes loading and unloading baggage from conveyor belts, ensuring proper placement on carts, and maintaining the integrity of each bag during transit. The baggage duty supervisor oversees the operations of baggage handlers and ensures compliance with all handling procedures. The ramp lead is responsible for coordinating baggage operations with aircraft crews and ensuring that all baggage is loaded and unloaded in accordance with safety and operational standards. All staff must report any discrepancies or incidents immediately and follow established procedures for resolution.

## 3. Handling Standards

All baggage handlers must inspect baggage for damage, leaks, or unattended status upon receipt. When a baggage handler finds a damaged, leaking, or unattended checked bag in the baggage make-up area, the handler must escalate the incident to the Baggage Duty Supervisor within 10 minutes of discovery and must report it under AP-INC-002. Heavy bags must be handled with care to prevent injury or damage. Fragile items must be clearly marked and handled with extra caution to ensure they are not subjected to unnecessary stress. All baggage must be properly secured and labeled to ensure accurate sorting and delivery.

## 4. Baggage Incident Escalation

In the event of a baggage incident, such as damage, loss, or delay, the baggage handler must immediately notify the Baggage Duty Supervisor. The supervisor is responsible for documenting the incident and initiating the appropriate corrective action. If the incident involves a potential safety hazard or significant delay, the supervisor must escalate the issue to the ramp lead for further coordination with the flight crew. All incidents must be reported in accordance with AP-INC-002, and affected passengers must be informed as soon as possible. The ramp lead is responsible for ensuring that all incidents are resolved in a timely and efficient manner to minimize disruption to flight operations.

## 5. Restricted Items Found in Baggage

If a restricted item is found in a bag during baggage handling, the baggage handler must immediately stop further processing of the bag and notify the Baggage Duty Supervisor. The bag must not be handled further until it is approved or removed under the restricted-items approval procedure in AP-SEC-003. The Baggage Duty Supervisor is responsible for ensuring that the bag is secured and that the appropriate authorities are notified. All restricted items must be reported and documented in accordance with applicable security policies.

## 6. Records and Review

All baggage incidents and restricted item findings must be recorded in the incident log for review and analysis. The incident log must be maintained by the Baggage Duty Supervisor and made available for inspection by authorized personnel. The policy will undergo an annual review to ensure its continued relevance and effectiveness. Any changes to the policy must be approved by the appropriate management and communicated to all affected staff.
