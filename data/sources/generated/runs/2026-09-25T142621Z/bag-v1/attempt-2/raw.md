# AP-BAG-001 Staff Baggage Handling and Escalation

## 1. Purpose and Scope  
This policy establishes procedures for the handling of checked baggage throughout the airport's baggage handling system. It outlines the responsibilities of staff involved in the movement and management of baggage from the check-in belts to the baggage make-up area and aircraft loading. The policy ensures the safe, efficient, and compliant handling of all baggage, including the proper identification, labeling, and treatment of items requiring special handling. It also defines the escalation process for baggage incidents to ensure timely resolution and compliance with applicable regulations. All staff must follow this policy and report any deviations or incidents promptly to maintain operational integrity and passenger satisfaction.

## 2. Roles and Responsibilities  
The Baggage Handler is responsible for the physical handling of baggage, including loading, unloading, and sorting. The Baggage Duty Supervisor oversees the operations of Baggage Handlers and ensures adherence to handling standards and safety protocols. The Ramp Lead is responsible for coordinating baggage handling activities with aircraft operations and ensuring that all baggage is loaded and unloaded in accordance with established procedures. All staff must follow this policy and report any deviations or incidents promptly. The Baggage Duty Supervisor must also ensure that all staff are trained and updated on the latest handling procedures and safety requirements.

## 3. Handling Standards  
Baggage must be handled with care to prevent damage during transit. All bags must be properly tagged with the correct destination and airline information. Heavy bags must be handled using appropriate equipment to avoid injury. Fragile items must be clearly marked and handled with extra care to prevent breakage. All baggage handlers must inspect bags for damage or unusual conditions before and after handling. Any discrepancies or concerns must be reported immediately to the Baggage Duty Supervisor for further action. The use of proper lifting techniques and equipment is mandatory to reduce the risk of musculoskeletal injuries and ensure the safety of all personnel.

## 4. Baggage Incident Escalation  
When a baggage handler finds a damaged, leaking, or unattended checked bag in the baggage make-up area, the handler must escalate the incident to the Baggage Duty Supervisor within 30 minutes of discovery and must report it under AP-INC-002. All baggage incidents must be documented and reported in a timely manner to ensure proper investigation and resolution. The Baggage Duty Supervisor is responsible for assessing the incident and determining the appropriate course of action. In the event of a serious incident, the Ramp Lead must be notified to coordinate with relevant departments for a swift response. The Baggage Duty Supervisor must ensure that all incident reports are completed accurately and submitted to the appropriate authorities for review and analysis.

## 5. Restricted Items Found in Baggage  
If a restricted item is found in a bag during baggage handling, the bag must not be further processed until it is approved or removed under the restricted-items approval procedure in AP-SEC-003. The Baggage Duty Supervisor is responsible for ensuring that all restricted items are properly identified and handled in accordance with applicable security and regulatory requirements. Any bag containing a restricted item must be isolated and marked for inspection until the appropriate authority has reviewed and authorized its further handling. The Baggage Duty Supervisor must also ensure that all staff are aware of the procedures for identifying and reporting restricted items to maintain the highest level of security and compliance.

## 6. Records and Review  
All baggage incidents must be recorded in the incident log for review and analysis. The incident log must include details such as the time, location, nature of the incident, and actions taken. The Baggage Duty Supervisor is responsible for maintaining and updating the incident log. This policy will be reviewed annually to ensure its continued relevance and effectiveness. Any changes to the policy must be communicated to all affected staff and implemented in accordance with established procedures. The incident log must be reviewed by the Ramp Lead and Baggage Duty Supervisor to identify trends and areas for improvement in baggage handling operations.
