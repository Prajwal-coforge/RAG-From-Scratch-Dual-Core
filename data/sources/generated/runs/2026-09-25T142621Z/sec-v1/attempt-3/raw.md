# AP-SEC-003 Staff Access, Restricted Items, and Approvals

## 1. Purpose and Scope  
This policy establishes procedures for managing staff access to restricted areas and the handling of restricted items within AeroPolicy Airport. It defines the zones where staff may operate, the types of items that require special handling, and the approval process for accessing these items. The policy ensures compliance with safety and security standards while maintaining operational efficiency. It applies to all staff members, including those working in landside, air, and baggage make-up areas. The procedures outlined are essential for mitigating risks associated with restricted items and ensuring proper authorization for their use. All staff must be familiar with this policy and adhere to its requirements to maintain a secure and compliant working environment.

## 2. Staff Access Zones  
Staff access to airport areas is restricted to designated zones based on job responsibilities. Landside areas include public spaces such as terminals and parking lots, accessible to all staff. Airside areas, including runways, taxiways, and aprons, are restricted to authorized personnel with proper credentials. The baggage make-up area is a controlled zone where only designated staff may operate. Access to these zones is governed by internal security protocols and must be verified through appropriate identification and authorization systems. Unauthorized entry into restricted zones is prohibited and subject to disciplinary action. Staff must ensure they are aware of their designated access zones and do not enter areas beyond their assigned responsibilities without prior approval.

## 3. Restricted Items  
Restricted items include tools, blades, lithium batteries, and flammable liquids, which pose potential safety or security risks. These items are subject to strict control measures to prevent misuse or accidental harm. Staff must be aware of the types of items that require special handling and understand the procedures for requesting access. The presence of restricted items in unauthorized locations may compromise safety and operational integrity. All staff are required to follow established protocols when dealing with such items to ensure compliance with regulatory and internal standards. Training on the identification and handling of restricted items is mandatory for all staff, and regular refresher sessions are conducted to maintain awareness and adherence to the policy.

## 4. Approval Procedure for Restricted Items  
Approval to carry a restricted item airside must be granted in writing by the Security Duty Manager before the item passes the staff screening point. A Requesting Staff Member must submit a written request detailing the item, its purpose, and the location of use. The Line Manager must endorse the request to confirm its necessity and compliance with operational requirements. The Security Duty, Manager reviews the endorsed request and grants final approval if the item meets safety and security criteria. All approvals must be documented and retained for audit purposes. Unauthorized possession or transport of restricted items is strictly prohibited and may result in disciplinary action. The approval process is designed to ensure that only necessary items are authorized for use in restricted areas, minimizing potential risks to personnel and operations.

## 5. Restricted Items Found in Baggage  
A restricted item found in a checked bag is handled under AP-BAG-001, which outlines procedures for identifying, isolating, and reporting such items. The discovery of a restricted item in baggage must be reported under AP-INC-002, ensuring proper documentation and escalation to the appropriate authorities. Staff are required to follow these procedures to ensure compliance with regulatory and internal standards. The handling of restricted items in baggage is a critical component of airport security and must be executed with diligence and accuracy to mitigate potential risks. All staff must be trained on the procedures outlined in AP-BAG-001 and AP-INC-002 to ensure timely and effective response to such incidents.

## 6. Records and Review  
All requests for restricted items must be recorded in a secure and accessible manner for audit and review. The records must include the date, item details, requesting staff member, and approval status. These records are subject to periodic review by the Security Duty Manager to ensure compliance with this policy and to identify any procedural gaps. Staff are required to maintain accurate and up-to-date records of all restricted item transactions. The review process supports continuous improvement in security and operational procedures, ensuring that all staff adhere to established guidelines. Regular audits are conducted to verify the accuracy and completeness of records, and any discrepancies are addressed promptly to maintain the integrity of the approval process.
